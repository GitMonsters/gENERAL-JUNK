# Pocket Guide Specifications - Arizona Super Highways

## Print Specs
- **Size:** 3.75" x 5.25" (Pocket Passport) - fits in wallet/back pocket
- **Pages:** 32-48 pages (perfect bound or saddle stitched)
- **Cover:** 120lb soft-touch matte cardstock with gold foil stamped highway-shield logo
- **Interior:** 80lb uncoated Accent Opaque (accepts ink stamps without bleed-through)
- **Binding:** Saddle-stitched with rounded corners
- **Print:** Full color CMYK
- **Finish:** Soft-touch cover, spot UV or foil on logo (optional)
- **Quantity (Initial):** 5,000-10,000

## Page Layout Structure

### Pages 1-2: Front/Inside Cover
- Logo, tagline

### Pages 3-8: Featured Locations
- **Featured:** Medusa Farms (Kingman, AZ) - full page
  - Logo, photos, hours, address, phone
  - QR code for check-in
  - Current specials

### Pages 9-16: Regional Directories
Organized by region:
- Northern AZ (Kingman, Flagstaff, Prescott)

### Pages 27-30: Community & Social

- App download badges (iOS/Android)

## QR Code Strategy
Each location gets unique QR codes:
- **Check-in QR:** `https://app.arizona-super-highways.com/c/{locationId}?t=checkin`
- Printed in guide, also on table tents/window decals
- Signed/encoded to prevent duplication
- Trackable per placement

## Content Guidelines
- No explicit consumption imagery in ads
- Comply with AZ cannabis advertising laws
- 21+ prominently displayed
- No medical claims
- Educational focus
- Include required disclaimers
