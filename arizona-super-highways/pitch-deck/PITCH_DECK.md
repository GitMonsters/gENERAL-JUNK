## Slide 2: Problem
- **Fragmented Market:** Arizona has 100+ dispensaries but no unified loyalty/rewards program
- **Poor Discovery:** Hard for tourists & locals to find deals across locations
- **Print Still Matters:** Cannabis marketing has heavy restrictions on digital advertising
- **Missed Opportunities:** Businesses struggle with customer retention
- **No Community:** Lack of platform for cannabis community in AZ

## Slide 3: Solution
**Arizona Super Highways** - A hybrid print + digital platform that:
- Connects the entire Arizona cannabis ecosystem
- Rewards exploration with a passport/stamp program
- Drives foot traffic to participating locations
- Creates a community hub for enthusiasts
- Bridges advertising restrictions with compliant marketing

## Slide 4: Product - Two-Sided Platform

### Physical: Pocket Passport Magazine
- 3.75" x 5.5" pocket guide
- Directory of all participating locations
- QR codes for instant check-in
- Advertisements drive revenue
- Distributed at locations & hotels

### Digital: Web + Mobile App
- Live map with geo-location
- Passport & tiered rewards
- Social feed & reviews
- Secure encrypted messaging
- Live video events
- E-commerce with Dutchie routing
