02 · capabilities

What survived the audit

61 files downloaded and read line-by-line. Coherent ideas were re-implemented clean-room; dead code and dimensionally incoherent claims were dropped. Every component below is trainable end-to-end and covered by tests.

◐

Causal tetrahedral attention

GitMonsters' per-head Gaussian relative bias, kept — near-lag heads track recent context, far-lag heads read deep history — but wrapped in a strict causal mask. The one-line fix that unblocks generation.

fixed · was the bug
⧉

Golden-ratio torus RoPE

Their two-band torus encoding aliased exactly: p and p+M got identical codes. Band B now winds at 1/φ per step — irrational, so no two positions ever repeat — applied as rotary rotations (relative, not absolute).

rebuilt · de-aliased
∿

Fractional limb mixer

8 Grünwald–Letnikov difference kernels of learned fractional order, depthwise over time. Upstream shipped it frozen under @torch.no_grad() with a centered (leaky) window — dead code. Now causal, learned end-to-end, and streamable with exact KV-cache parity.

fixed · was dead code
⌘

Lossless byte-BPE tokenizer

Replaces their word-level tokenizer that mapped 23% of the eval corpus to <UNK>. Byte-level base alphabet makes unks impossible; whitespace runs survive byte-for-byte — validated on adversarial Unicode, emoji, tabs & newlines.

rebuilt · zero <unk>
◈

Honest evaluation loop

Every PPL in this project is strictly causal — same mask as training and generation, enforced by a test. The world-model auxiliary loss from their six-term objective survives at λ=0.05; the no-grad terms that never trained anything were dropped.

rebuilt · measured, not claimed
⚡

Exact KV-cache generation

Attention cache + fractional-mixer rolling history reproduce the full forward pass to 2×10⁻⁵ max error on trained weights — validated token-by-token across 64 positions. CPU inference, no approximations.

validated · 11/11 checks

The signature, predicted and confirmed: the leaky model wins only under its own non-causal measurement (1.006 vs 1.025), descends to loss 0.067 (PPL ≈ 1.07) six times faster than the causal model needs, and its advantage vanishes the instant the mask is forced on (1.133 — worse than the causal model's 1.025). That is precisely the anatomy of target leakage, reproduced from their code.
