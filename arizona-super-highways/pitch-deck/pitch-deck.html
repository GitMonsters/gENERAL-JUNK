04 · How It Works

From paper to purchase.

1

Discover

Traveler picks up the passport at a hotel, dispensary, or event. Every listing has a signed QR code.

2

Check in

Scan at the counter. The app verifies the signature, adds a stamp, and awards points instantly.

3

Unlock

Tier-gated offers, BOGO deals, swag, and hidden drops appear in the wallet based on stamps collected.

4

Order

Cart routes to the closest partner with live Dutchie inventory. Taxes calculated for that jurisdiction.

5

Return

Points, badges, reviews, and event invites pull the member back — to this store or the next stop on the highway.

Attribution loop

Every printed page has a unique code, so the partner sees exactly which placement produced the visit.

12 · Compliance & Trust

Built for ADHS from day one.

Regulatory posture

  • 21+ age gate in print, web, and app
  • No cannabis payment processing inside app-store wrappers
  • All advertising reviewed against Arizona cannabis marketing rules
  • Partner license verification before listing
  • No medical claims; education-first content

Data & security

  • Age/ID verification via a third-party provider
  • Signal-style end-to-end encryption for member messaging
  • Aggregate-only reporting to partners — no member PII
  • Signed, single-location QR codes to prevent fraud
  • Audit logging for redemptions and routed orders

Positioning: Arizona Super Highways is a directory, loyalty, and discovery platform — not a retailer. Orders route to licensed partners, and payment happens in their compliant flow.
