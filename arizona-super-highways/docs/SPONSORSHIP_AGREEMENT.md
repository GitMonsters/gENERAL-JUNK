## 1. Parties

**Publisher:** Arizona Super Highways LLC ("Publisher"), an Arizona limited liability company.
**Partner:** Medusa Farms ("Partner"), an ADHS-licensed cannabis establishment located at 3490 N Bank Street, Kingman, AZ 86409.

Term: **12 months** from the Effective Date, with a 60-day renewal notice.

## 2. Purpose

Publisher produces a pocket-sized printed passport directory and a companion web/mobile
application that drive verified foot traffic to participating Arizona cannabis and wellness
businesses. Partner becomes the **Anchor Partner** for the Western / Route 66 district and
the featured demo location for the network launch.

## 3. Sponsorship Tier

Partner elects the **Headline Sponsor** package.

| Package | Monthly | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Base Directory | $500 | Directory listing, live map pin, standard profile, 1 offer |
| Highway Tier | $1,500 | Full page in print guide, featured Dutchie integration, 3 offers, 2 push notifications/month |
| **Headline Sponsor** | **$3,500** | Cover logo, first-position listing, exclusive secret-drop slot, event/live-stream sponsorship, 6 offers, unlimited push, quarterly analytics |

Selected tier: **☒ Headline Sponsor — $3,500/month** (12-month minimum = $42,000)

## 4. Publisher Deliverables

1. **Print**
   - Anchor placement in the Western / Route 66 section of every edition.
   - Partner logo on the cover (Headline tier) and a dedicated full-page profile.
   - Unique, signed QR code assigned to the Partner for check-in attribution.
   - 500 complimentary pocket guides per quarter for in-store distribution.
2. **App / Web**
   - Verified location profile: address, hours, phone, website, photos, offers.
   - Live menu integration through Dutchie (subject to Partner's Dutchie account and API access).
   - Passport check-in campaign with Partner-branded reward (e.g., +50 pts first visit).
   - One exclusive "secret drop" slot per quarter, tier-gated to passport members.
   - Two push notifications per month (content approved by Partner, 48-hour review).
   - Encrypted member-to-business messaging inbox with moderation tools.
3. **Events**
   - Co-hosted workshop or live stream once per quarter (Partner provides venue/talent;
     Publisher provides production, promotion, and streaming).
4. **Reporting**
   - Monthly dashboard: scans, unique check-ins, offer redemptions, routed orders,
     and anonymized audience stats (tier mix, city, visit frequency).
   - Quarterly performance review with recommendations.

## 5. Partner Obligations

1. Maintain active ADHS licensing and good standing; provide license number on request.
2. Honor published passport offers for the stated period; notify Publisher ≥7 days before
   changing or ending an offer.
3. Redeem offers only through the app/QR flow so attribution is accurate.
4. Provide accurate menu, pricing, and inventory data (directly or via Dutchie).
5. Comply with all Arizona cannabis advertising rules: 21+ targeting, no medical claims,
   no appeals to minors, required warnings.
6. Approve creative within 5 business days; silence = approval.

## 6. Fees & Payment

- **Sponsorship:** $3,500/month, invoiced on the 1st, net 15.
- **Print overage:** additional guides at $1.25 each beyond the 500/quarter allowance.
- **Delivery routing:** Publisher receives **3% of subtotal** (excluding tax and delivery fee)
  on orders routed to Partner through the app. Invoiced monthly against the Dutchie
  order report; Partner may audit with 30 days' notice.
- **Booklet sales:** Partner may resell guides at $19.99 MSRP and retains 40% of cover price;
  Publisher retains 60%. Unsold guides are non-returnable after 90 days.
- Late payments accrue 1.5%/month. Sponsorship may pause after 30 days past due.

## 7. Exclusivity

- Partner receives **category exclusivity within a 15-mile radius of 3490 N Bank Street**
  for the Western / Route 66 district during the term, limited to cannabis retail.
- Publisher may still list non-competing wellness, treatment, and event partners.
- Exclusivity does not apply to brand advertisers or event sponsors.

## 8. Data, Privacy & Attribution

- Check-in data is collected with member consent and reported only in aggregate.
- Partner receives no member PII, contact lists, or encrypted message contents.
- QR codes are signed and single-location; tampering voids attribution.
- Partner may use Publisher-provided performance stats in its own marketing with attribution.

## 9. Brand & Creative

- Partner grants Publisher a limited license to use its name, logo, and approved photography
  solely to promote the network during the term.
- Publisher grants Partner a "Passport Partner" badge for in-store and digital use.
- All print and digital creative is subject to Partner approval and Arizona compliance review.

## 10. Compliance

Both parties will comply with the Arizona Department of Health Services rules (A.A.C. R9-17),
Arizona Revised Statutes Title 36, Chapter 28.2, and applicable local ordinances.
Publisher's app enforces a 21+ gate and does not process cannabis payments. Nothing in this
agreement requires Partner to violate law or licensing conditions. If a deliverable becomes
non-compliant, the parties will substitute a compliant alternative in good faith.

## 11. Term, Renewal & Termination

- **Initial term:** 12 months. Auto-renews for 12 months unless either party gives 60 days' notice.
- **For cause:** either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice for uncured material
  breach (15 days to cure), or immediately for license loss, fraud, or illegal conduct.
- **Convenience:** Publisher may terminate on 90 days' notice; prepaid unused sponsorship
  is credited pro rata.
- **Effect:** Partner's listing and QR attribution end at term; outstanding offers must be
  honored for 30 days. Sections 6 (accrued fees), 8, 12, and 13 survive.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

Publisher's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid in the preceding 3 months.
Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
Publisher does not guarantee sales, foot traffic, or order volume; it guarantees delivery
of the contracted placements and reporting.

## 13. Confidentiality & IP

- Each party keeps the other's non-public business information confidential for 2 years.
- Publisher owns the guide, app, passport program, and member data.
- Partner owns its trademarks, menu content, and photography.
- Feedback and anonymized performance data may be used by Publisher to improve the network.
